Who made this, where to find it, and who contributed the data for the board you are looking at.

---

The tab carries:

* Links to the **GitHub project page**, the **helper page** at
  [classic-repair-toolbox.dk](https://classic-repair-toolbox.dk) and the **YouTube channel**
* The date the **board Excel data** you are using was last revised — useful when you are wondering
  whether a correction has reached you yet
* **Credits for this board specifically**: the people who gathered and checked its data

The credits change with the selected board, since the data for each one was contributed by different
people.

If you have contributed data for a board, this is where your name appears — see
[Contribute data via CRT](Contribute-data-via-CRT).
