Send an issue, a suggestion or a question straight to the developer.

---

Type your message under **Your feedback** and click **Send feedback**. That is the whole tab. The
message is required; everything else is optional.

**Email address** is optional - but without it there is no way to reply to you, so include it if you
want an answer. If you are a maintainer signed in on the [Maintainer tab](Maintainer-tab), it is
your account's address and cannot be changed here - also while that tab is turned off or hidden; sign
out there to use another one.

Use it for anything about the application itself: something that does not work, something that could
be better, or a question the documentation did not answer.

**Attachments** help with anything that goes wrong:

* **Attach application logfile and crash reports** - CRT's log file, and the crash log if CRT has
  crashed
* **Attach configuration and traces data files** - your settings file, and the file holding the
  traces you have drawn yourself on the schematics
* **Attach file(s)** and **Attach folder** - files or folders of your own, a screenshot say. They
  are listed below the buttons; remove one with its bin icon, or all of them with
  **Clear attachments**.

Packed together the attachments may be at most 250 MB; CRT says so before sending if they are
larger.

## What not to use it for

**Wrong or missing board data** - a part number, a datasheet link, a component highlight in the
wrong place. Change it in CRT instead, so the actual data change is sent rather than a description
of it: make the change in the [Contribute tab](Contribute-tab), which saves it to a local draft, and
send it with **Submit** on the [Drafts tab](Drafts-tab).

**A whole new board** - create it in CRT with **Add a new board** and send it from the
[Drafts tab](Drafts-tab). [Contribute data via CRT](Contribute-data-via-CRT) walks through both.
